Web Design & Development in Minneapolis, MN

A Minneapolis web design company that builds for results, not just looks

X-Traordinary Development is a Black-owned, Minneapolis-based web design and development studio. We build websites for Twin Cities businesses that need to be found on Google, look credible on a phone, and turn visitors into phone calls, bookings, and estimates. No templates, no page builders – every site is designed and hand-coded around what your business actually needs to do.

We have built sites for a licensed Minnesota general contractor, a Minneapolis charity gala, a Saint John's Abbey art auction, and a statewide mobile-fueling company. We know the Twin Cities market: homeowners searching for remodelers, donors buying gala tickets, and B2B buyers vetting a vendor before they call. Your website is built for the people in your market, not a generic template audience.

Whether you are a small business in Northeast Minneapolis, a nonprofit in Saint Paul, or a growing company in Edina, Bloomington, Plymouth, or Maple Grove, we meet in person when it helps and work remotely when it is faster. Most projects are quoted as a fixed price, so you know the number before we start.

What every Minneapolis website from us includes

The essentials that make a site rank, load fast, and convert – built in, not upsold.

Custom design around your brand

Layout, color, typography, and imagery chosen for your business and your customers – not a lookalike of the other contractors, clinics, or shops in your neighborhood.

Local SEO foundations

Title tags and headings written for what Twin Cities customers actually search, LocalBusiness structured data, an XML sitemap, fast mobile load times, and clean semantic HTML so Google can read every page.

Conversion built in

Click-to-call buttons, estimate and booking forms, Google review integration, and clear calls-to-action placed where people decide. We measure form submits and phone taps so you know what works.

You own everything

Your code, your content, your domain. Host it with us or anywhere you like. No monthly ransom, no builder lock-in, and full documentation when we hand it over.

How a website project works with us

We start with a short call about your goals – more estimate requests, more ticket sales, more qualified leads – and who your customers are. From that we write a fixed-price proposal with a page list, timeline, and everything included.

Design and build happen together. You see real pages on a real link within days, not mockups you have to imagine. Simple local business sites launch in about a week; larger multi-page sites typically take 3–6 weeks depending on content, photography, and integrations like scheduling, payments, or a CRM.

After launch we submit the site to Google Search Console, set up analytics and conversion tracking, and walk you through editing your own content. Ongoing support is optional, never required.

Minneapolis web design questions

How do you quote a website project?

After a short call about your goals and the pages you need, we send a written proposal with a fixed price, a page list, and a timeline – so you know exactly what is included before any work starts. No hourly surprises and no retainers.

Do you meet clients in person in the Twin Cities?

Yes. We are based in Minneapolis and are happy to meet in person anywhere in the metro – Minneapolis, Saint Paul, Bloomington, Edina, Eden Prairie, Plymouth, Maple Grove – or work entirely remotely if that is easier for you.

Will my site show up when people search for my business in Minneapolis?

Every site ships with local SEO foundations: keyword-targeted titles and headings, LocalBusiness structured data, a sitemap submitted to Google, and fast mobile performance. We also help you set up or clean up your Google Business Profile, which drives the map results.

Can you redesign my existing website instead of starting over?

Yes. If your current site is slow, dated, or not converting, we can rebuild it on a clean codebase while preserving the URLs and content that already rank. See our website redesign service for how that works.

What if I need more than a website – like a customer portal or an app?

That is our other specialty. We build custom software, web applications, and AI tools for Minneapolis companies, so your website can grow into a real product when you are ready.
