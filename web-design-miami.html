Web Design & Development in Miami, FL

Custom websites for Miami businesses that compete on first impressions

Miami is a visual market. Restaurants, real estate, events, medical and aesthetic practices, boutique fitness, and the startups around Brickell and Wynwood all sell on how they look before a customer ever calls. X-Traordinary Development builds custom websites for South Florida businesses that look the part on a phone, load fast on a cell connection, and are built to be found on Google.

We are a Minneapolis-founded, Black-owned development studio serving Miami clients remotely and on site. Our team builds everything from single-page launch sites to multi-page marketing websites, event platforms with ticketing, and full web applications. Every project is hand-coded – no templates, no page builders – and priced as a fixed number before we begin.

Because a large share of Miami customers search and read in Spanish, we design with bilingual content in mind: language toggles, properly tagged Spanish pages that Google indexes separately, and layouts that hold up when the copy runs longer. If your business serves Miami-Dade and Broward in two languages, your website should too.

What a Miami website from us includes

Built for how South Florida customers actually find and judge a business.

Design that matches the market

Bold imagery, video, and motion where it earns attention – restrained where credibility matters, like medical, legal, and financial services. Designed for your brand, not adapted from a theme.

Local SEO for Miami-Dade & Broward

Titles, headings, and structured data targeted to how people search in Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral, and Fort Lauderdale, plus Google Business Profile setup so you appear in the map results.

Bilingual-ready structure

Optional Spanish versions of your key pages with correct hreflang tags, so both English- and Spanish-speaking customers find you and Google treats each version properly.

Fast on mobile data

Compressed images and video, lazy loading, and a lean codebase so the site is quick on a phone on the causeway, not just on office Wi-Fi. Speed is a Google ranking factor and a conversion factor.

Working with us from Miami

Most of our Miami projects run remotely with video calls and a shared project link where you can watch real pages come together. When a project benefits from being on site – a photo shoot, a launch, a workshop with your team – we are there.

You get a fixed-price proposal with a page list and timeline before work starts. Simple local business sites launch in about a week; multi-page custom sites typically take 3–6 weeks depending on content, photography, and integrations like reservations, booking, or payments.

After launch we submit the site to Google, set up analytics and conversion tracking, and show you how to update content yourself. Ongoing support and hosting are available but optional – you own the site outright.

Miami web design questions

Do you work with businesses in Miami if you are based in Minneapolis?

Yes. We serve Miami and South Florida clients remotely and on site. Design reviews, revisions, and launch all happen through a shared link and video calls, and we travel for projects that benefit from being in the room.

Can you build a bilingual English and Spanish website?

Yes. We build Spanish versions of your key pages with correct hreflang tags and a language toggle, so Google indexes each language properly and every customer lands on the version they read.

How do you quote a Miami website project?

After a short call about your business and the pages you need, we send a fixed-price proposal with a page list and timeline. Multi-page custom sites for Miami businesses – restaurants, real estate, events, medical practices – are scoped based on pages and integrations, never billed hourly.

Can you integrate reservations, booking, or ticketing?

Yes. We integrate reservation and booking systems, event ticketing, payment processing, and CRM tools, and we have built complete event platforms with ticket sales and sponsorship funnels from scratch.

Will my site rank in Miami search results?

Every site includes local SEO foundations targeted to Miami-Dade and Broward searches, structured data, a sitemap submitted to Google, and fast mobile performance. We also set up your Google Business Profile, which drives the map listings people click first.

Tell us about the business and what the site needs to do. You will get a fixed price and a real timeline – in English or Spanish.
