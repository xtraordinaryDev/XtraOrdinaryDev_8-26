Built by a developer who spent years inside companies like yours

The development team for companies that don’t have one

X-traordinary Development was started by Xavier Thurman after years building internal applications inside large companies, including U.S. Bank and CHS Inc. The pattern was the same everywhere: the companies that needed software most were the ones without anyone to build it. An IT leader, a budget, a spreadsheet process three people ran by hand, and no developer.

So that is who we build for. Mid-sized companies, 200 to 2,000 people, in engineering, agriculture, manufacturing, distribution and professional services. We are the entire development function for a 1,000-person Minnesota engineering firm today, and we build everything inside the client’s own Microsoft and Azure environment, so what we make is theirs from the first commit.

We stopped building websites in 2026 to do this one thing well. We don’t sell hours, we don’t run pilots that go nowhere, and we don’t start building until a two-week assessment has told us what’s worth building. As a Black-owned business rooted in Minneapolis and Miami, we bring the same standard to every client: quality code, plain communication, and a system your people actually use.

Our Mission

Give every mid-sized company without a development team one it can trust: a partner that knows their business, builds inside their environment, and stays for years, at a price under one developer’s salary.

Our Vision

Start with the process that costs the most hours, not the most impressive demo. Keep a person in the loop on anything that matters. Keep the data in the client’s tenant. Ship something real in the first six weeks, then keep shipping every quarter.
