About X-Traordinary Development

More Than Developers

X-Traordinary Development was founded with a clear belief: technology should serve people — not the other way around. Based in Minneapolis, MN and serving clients in Miami, FL and across the US, we are a custom software and AI development partner built on transparency, quality craftsmanship, and genuine investment in your success.

We started X-Traordinary because we saw too many businesses stuck between overpriced agencies and unreliable freelancers. There was a gap for a partner that was technically excellent, deeply honest, and truly committed to client outcomes. That’s the company we set out to build — and it’s the standard we hold ourselves to on every project.

Our team brings hands-on experience across industries — from logistics and healthcare to retail, SaaS, and financial services — delivering everything from custom web applications and mobile apps to AI-powered solutions and DevOps transformations. As a proud Black-owned business rooted in the Minneapolis community, we bring that same spirit of resilience, excellence, and community investment into everything we do.

Whether you’re launching your first digital product, scaling an existing platform, or rescuing a troubled codebase, we show up with quality code, clear communication, and outcomes that actually move your business forward.

Our Mission

X-Traordinary is dedicated to tackling complex business challenges and leveraging technology to provide effective solutions. The approach goes beyond mere transactions, prioritizing personalized relationships built on trust and consistent value delivery. At X-Traordinary, we empower businesses through technology.

Our Vision

At X-Traordinary, we believe the promise of technology is boundless, but complicated with technical jargon and unknowns. We aim to simplify complex operations, create meaningful user experiences, and deliver scalable, enduring business value. We believe success is a combination of satisfied customers, fulfilled employees, and useful solutions. Everything we do is intended to find the right balance of outcomes for the common benefit of all.
