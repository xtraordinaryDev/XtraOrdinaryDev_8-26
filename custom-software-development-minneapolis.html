Custom Software Development in Minneapolis, MN

Software built for how your Minneapolis business actually runs

Off-the-shelf software makes you change your process to fit the tool. Custom software does the opposite. X-Traordinary Development designs and builds web applications, internal tools, customer portals, dashboards, and AI systems for Minneapolis and Twin Cities companies that have outgrown spreadsheets, duct-taped SaaS subscriptions, or a legacy system nobody wants to touch.

Our engineers have shipped internal applications inside U.S. Bank and multiple internal tools for CHS Inc., a Fortune 100 agricultural cooperative headquartered in Inver Grove Heights – environments where uptime, security, and code review are non-negotiable. We bring that discipline to mid-size Minnesota businesses without the enterprise price tag or the six-month discovery phase.

We work across .NET, React, Angular, Python, and modern cloud infrastructure, and we have delivered for logistics, healthcare, retail, SaaS, financial services, and construction clients. Based in Minneapolis, we can sit down with your team in the metro or run the whole engagement remotely.

How a custom software project works

We begin with a working session to map the process you want to improve and the outcome you want – hours saved, errors eliminated, orders processed, customers served. You get a written scope with a fixed price for a defined first release, not an open-ended hourly estimate.

We build in short cycles and put working software in front of your team early, usually within the first two weeks. Feedback from the people who will actually use the system shapes every release. Security, automated testing, and documentation are part of the build, not a line item added at the end.

After launch we can hand the system to your internal team with full documentation, or stay on for support, hosting, and new features. Either way, you own the source code and the infrastructure.

Custom software questions from Minneapolis businesses

How do you scope and quote a software project?

We define a focused first release – an internal tool, a customer portal, or an MVP – and quote it as a fixed price for that scope. Larger platforms are broken into fixed-price phases so you always know what the next step includes before committing to it.

How long does it take to build custom software?

Most first releases ship in 4–12 weeks. We put working software in front of your team within the first two weeks and release improvements continuously rather than making you wait months for a big reveal.

Which technologies do you use?

.NET and C#, React and Next.js, Angular, Python, SQL Server and PostgreSQL, and cloud infrastructure on Azure, AWS, and Vercel. We choose the stack that fits your existing systems and your team's ability to maintain it.

Can you take over an existing application another developer built?

Yes. Project takeovers are a core part of our work. We audit the codebase, stabilize what is running, document it, and then modernize it in steps – no risky rewrite from scratch unless it is genuinely the cheaper path.

Do you work with enterprise and regulated industries?

We have engineered internal applications at U.S. Bank and CHS Inc. and built systems for healthcare, logistics, and financial services clients. Security reviews, access controls, and audit trails are standard in how we build.

Have a process that software should be doing for you?

Describe the problem in a few sentences. We will tell you honestly whether custom software is the right answer and what a first release would cost.
