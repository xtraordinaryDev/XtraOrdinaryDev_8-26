# Meta ads, premium set: copy, founder video script, launch plan

Target: $4,500+ website projects for Minnesota contractors and local businesses that charge premium prices.

Campaign in Ads Manager: `META_Leads_MN-Contractors_LeadEngine_Sep26` (id 120256168051680273), ad set B "/start Landing Page" (id 120256168053640273), Minnesota, 25–60, Advantage+ audience. Destination for every ad:

`https://x-traordinarydevelopment.com/start?utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=leadengine&utm_content=<ad name>`

---

## How high-ticket ads differ from the last set

- **State the price floor in the ad.** "From $4,500" costs you cheap leads and buys you serious ones. Expect cost per lead to roughly double versus a no-price ad, and cost per *qualified* lead to fall. Judge only on qualified leads (budget answer of $5,000+ on the form).
- **Show the most refined work, not the most relatable.** The bargain set showed a contractor site because it was relatable. This set opens on VSC Peptides and Plates & Playmakers because they look expensive, then lands on The R3D Key to prove it books estimates.
- **Restraint reads as premium.** Smaller type, more space, no badges, no strikethroughs, no "cheap," "affordable," or "deal." Gold hairlines instead of gold blocks.
- **Scarcity that's true.** "Four websites a month" is a real capacity statement, not a countdown timer. Only say it if it's true. If you can do six, say six.
- **Same story on the page.** `/start` currently shows a $2,500 Starter tier. A visitor who saw "from $4,500" and finds $2,500 either feels baited or buys the cheap one. Decide before launch: remove Starter, or raise it to $4,500 and keep Lead Engine at $6,500+ and Authority at $9,500+.

## Launch plan

1. **Fix the page first.** Decide the Starter tier question above. Submit the `/start` form once with a $5,000+ budget and confirm `Lead` and `SubmitApplication` fire in Events Manager.
2. **Budget question on the form.** Keep it required. It's your filter. The GHL automation routes under-$2k to the low-budget path automatically.
3. **Launch P1, P2 (both sizes), S1, S2, S3** in ad set B at $40/day. Leave it alone for 5 days.
4. **After $150 per ad:** pause the bottom two by cost per qualified lead, not cost per lead.
5. **Add the founder video** as soon as it's shot. If it wins, cut two more with the alternate first lines.
6. **After 30 `Lead` events**, switch optimization to `SubmitApplication`. With the price in the ad this can happen sooner than the spec assumed.
7. Budget moves of 20% or less, three days apart.

## Expectations

Home-services leads on Meta average about $34. A qualified $4,500+ web project lead will cost more, likely $60–120. At a 20% close rate that's $300–600 per $4,500+ project, which is the right trade. If cost per qualified lead runs past $150 for two weeks, the fix is usually the founder video or the landing page, not the targeting.

## What I did NOT make

- No AI people, voices or testimonials.
- No before/after ad. There are no real "before" screenshots yet. Once the three spec mockups exist, that ad is ten minutes of work and will likely be the best static in the set.
- No result numbers. Use one only when it's true and you can name the client.
