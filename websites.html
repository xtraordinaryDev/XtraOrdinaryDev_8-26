Websites That Do More Than Look Good

Your website is often the first real conversation your customers have with you. We design and build sites that feel fast, trustworthy, and intentional – so visitors don’t just land, they stay, explore, and convert.

Built for Real Business Goals

We don’t ship generic templates. Every website starts with your goals – bookings, leads, sales, or education – and is designed to guide people toward meaningful action. Clear structure, intuitive navigation, and thoughtful CTAs are baked in from day one.

From small business sites to multi-page marketing experiences, we keep things simple to manage on your side and frictionless to use on the customer’s side.

Modern, Accessible, and Fast

Great websites feel effortless. We focus on performance, accessibility, and responsive design so your site loads quickly, reads clearly, and looks sharp on any device. No bloated plugins, no mystery code – just clean, maintainable foundations.

We also follow best practices for SEO and structure so search engines – and real people – can easily understand what you do.

How much does a custom website cost?

Our fixed-price local business website package is $399 and launches in about 7 days. Larger multi-page custom sites, e-commerce stores, and web applications are quoted individually – always as a fixed price with no agency retainers.

How long does it take to build a custom website?

Local business sites launch in roughly a week. Multi-page custom websites typically take 3–6 weeks depending on the number of pages, content readiness, and integrations like booking, payments, or CRM.

Do you use templates or page builders?

No. Every X-Traordinary website is hand-coded and designed around your brand and goals. No bloated themes, no page-builder lock-in – just clean, fast, maintainable code you own.

Will my website be optimized for Google?

Yes. Technical SEO is built in: mobile-first responsive design, fast load times, clean semantic HTML, structured data, XML sitemaps, and metadata on every page so search engines and customers can find you.

Do you work with businesses outside Minneapolis?

Yes. We are based in Minneapolis, MN and also serve Miami, FL, and we work remotely with clients across the United States.

Do I own my website when it is finished?

Completely. You own the code, the design, the content, and the domain. We can host it for you or hand it off to any hosting provider you choose.
