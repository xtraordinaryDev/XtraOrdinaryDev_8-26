What We Offer

  • Mobile Development

    Design and build fast, secure mobile apps for iOS and Android — from MVPs to production-ready products — using modern, scalable architectures. Whether you need a customer-facing app, an internal enterprise tool, or a marketplace platform, our team handles UX design, development, testing, and App Store deployment end-to-end. We use React Native and native Swift/Kotlin to deliver performant, polished experiences that users love and businesses depend on. Serving mobile app clients in Minneapolis, Miami, and across the US.

  • Web Development

    Create high-performance marketing sites, web apps, and dashboards that look great on every device and are easy for your team to manage. We specialize in React, Angular, and .NET-powered applications with clean UI, fast load times, and accessibility built in from day one. From e-commerce stores and SaaS platforms to internal portals and landing pages, every project is built with SEO, performance, and long-term maintainability in mind. Trusted by businesses in Minneapolis, MN, Miami, FL, and beyond.

  • Custom Development

    Turn complex ideas into tailored software — integrations, internal tools, and automation built around your exact workflows and goals. We work closely with your team to deeply understand your processes before writing a single line of code, so the solution fits your business rather than forcing your business to fit the software. Our custom work spans API development, third-party integrations (Salesforce, Stripe, HubSpot, and more), workflow automation, and bespoke applications across industries from healthcare to logistics to finance.

  • AI Solutions

    Leverage machine learning, automation, and data-driven insights to streamline operations, personalize experiences, and uncover new opportunities. Our AI work ranges from building custom language model pipelines and computer vision systems to integrating OpenAI, AWS AI, and Azure Cognitive Services into your existing platforms. We deliver practical, production-ready AI — tools your team can actually use and that deliver measurable ROI — not experiments. Helping businesses in Minneapolis, MN and Miami, FL adopt AI that works.

  • Project Takeovers

    Rescue stalled or messy projects with clear audits, refactoring, and a roadmap to get you back on track without starting from scratch. We’ve stepped into codebases of all sizes and conditions — inheriting legacy systems, clearing out technical debt, and picking up where other teams left off. Our takeover process starts with a comprehensive technical and code audit, followed by a prioritized plan to stabilize, modernize, and scale your existing software. You protect your investment; we help it reach its full potential.

  • .NET Development

    Build robust, enterprise-grade applications and APIs on .NET with clean architectures, strong security, and long-term maintainability. Our team has deep expertise across the full .NET ecosystem — ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework, Blazor, SignalR, and Azure — delivering solutions that meet enterprise compliance, performance, and scalability requirements. Whether you’re migrating from legacy .NET Framework or building a greenfield microservices architecture, we deliver structured, well-tested, production-ready code built to last.

  • DevOps Consulting

    Improve reliability and release velocity with CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, observability, and automation that fits your stack. We help engineering teams ship faster and more confidently by building automated testing workflows, GitHub Actions and Azure DevOps pipelines, Docker and Kubernetes container orchestration, and cloud infrastructure on AWS and Azure. Our engagements also include monitoring, alerting, and runbook creation so your team is prepared for anything in production — day or night.

  • Python Development

    Use Python for data processing, APIs, automations, and AI-powered features that connect seamlessly with your existing systems. We build Django and FastAPI backends, data engineering pipelines, scripting and automation tools, and ML model integrations that solve real business problems. Python’s flexibility makes it our go-to for rapid prototyping and production-grade AI workloads alike — from ETL pipelines processing millions of records to chatbots, recommendation engines, and predictive analytics dashboards.

  • Angular Development

    Develop structured, scalable front-end applications with Angular, including component libraries, dashboards, and internal enterprise tools. Angular’s opinionated architecture gives large teams a consistent foundation for building complex SPAs that are maintainable and extensible over time. We apply Angular best practices — lazy loading, reactive forms, RxJS state management, NgRx, and strict TypeScript — to deliver fast, reliable, enterprise-quality front-end applications built to grow with your team.

  • React Development

    Ship fast, interactive user interfaces in React — from landing pages to complex SPAs — with reusable components and performance best practices. React is our primary front-end framework for modern web applications, and our team uses it daily across projects ranging from marketing sites to data-heavy dashboards and multi-tenant SaaS platforms. We pair React with TypeScript, Next.js for server-side rendering, and state solutions like Redux or Zustand to build interfaces that are fast, accessible, and easy for your product team to continuously improve.
