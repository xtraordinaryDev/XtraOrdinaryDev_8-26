Website Redesign Services

A redesign should make you faster and easier to find – not reset you to zero

Most website redesigns fail in one of two ways: the new site looks better but loads slower and converts worse, or the agency changes every URL and the business quietly loses the Google rankings it spent years earning. X-Traordinary Development redesigns websites so neither happens.

We rebuild your site on clean, hand-coded HTML and CSS – no bloated theme, no page-builder plugins – while preserving every URL and piece of content that already ranks. Old addresses get permanent redirects, page titles and headings are rewritten for what customers actually search, and the whole thing is tested on real phones before it goes live.

Redesigns are a good fit if your site is more than three or four years old, was built on WordPress or a page builder and has gotten slow, does not look right on mobile, or simply is not producing calls and form submissions anymore. We work with businesses in Minneapolis and Miami and remotely across the United States.

What a redesign with us includes

Everything needed to launch the new site without losing what the old one earned.

SEO-safe migration

A full inventory of your current URLs, rankings, and backlinks before anything changes. Every old address 301-redirects to its new home, metadata is rewritten for search, and the sitemap is resubmitted to Google on launch day.

Speed & Core Web Vitals

Modern image formats, lazy loading, optimized video, and a lean codebase. We target Google's Core Web Vitals thresholds on mobile – the numbers that actually affect ranking – and show you the before-and-after scores.

Mobile-first design

Most of your visitors are on a phone. Layouts, tap targets, forms, and calls-to-action are designed for a 390-pixel screen first, then scaled up to desktop – not the other way around.

Conversion cleanup

Clear next steps on every page: click-to-call, quote and booking forms, reviews, and proof. We wire up analytics and conversion tracking so you can see whether the redesign is producing more leads than the old site.

How the redesign process works

We start with an audit of your existing site: speed, mobile usability, search rankings, broken links, and where visitors are leaving. You get a plain-English summary of what is holding the site back and a fixed-price proposal for the rebuild.

Design and development happen on a private link, so your current site stays live and untouched until the new one is finished. You review real pages on your own phone, not static mockups.

Launch is a single switchover with redirects, analytics, and Search Console all in place. We monitor rankings and errors for the following weeks and fix anything that moves. You own the code and can edit content yourself afterward.

Website redesign questions

Will a redesign hurt my Google rankings?

Not when it is done properly. We map every existing URL, keep or redirect each one, carry over the content that ranks, and resubmit the sitemap at launch. Done carelessly – changing URLs without redirects, deleting pages, slowing the site down – a redesign absolutely can hurt rankings, which is exactly what we prevent.

How is a website redesign quoted?

After a short audit of your current site we send a fixed-price proposal that lists every page, redirect, and integration included. Small business sites are scoped like a new build; larger sites with many pages or integrations are scoped page by page so nothing is missed.

Can you redesign a WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, or GoDaddy site?

Yes. We regularly move sites off page builders and heavy WordPress themes onto clean, hand-coded pages that load faster and are easier to maintain. Your content and images come with you; the bloat does not.

How long does a website redesign take?

Most redesigns launch in 2–6 weeks depending on the number of pages and whether content or photography needs updating. Your existing site stays live the entire time.

Do I need to rewrite all my content?

Usually not. We keep what is working, tighten headlines and calls-to-action, and rewrite page titles and descriptions for search. If you want new copy or photography we can help, but it is not required for a successful redesign.
