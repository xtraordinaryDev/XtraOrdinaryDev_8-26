Most companies between 200 and 2,000 people are in the same spot. The managed IT provider runs the network. The ERP runs the books. Everything in between runs on spreadsheets, email and three people who know how. We build the software that closes that gap, and we keep building it, inside the Microsoft and Azure environment you already pay for.

A fit if

  • 200 to 2,000 people, no in-house developers
  • Engineering, construction, agriculture, manufacturing, distribution or professional services
  • Microsoft 365, Azure or a plan to get there

Not a fit if

  • You need a website or a brochure

What we build

“AI-native” in the words your team would use

Five kinds of things, always inside your tenant, always with a person approving. We start with the process that costs you the most hours, not the most impressive demo.

An assistant over your own documents

“Ask it anything about our standards, contracts and past projects, and it answers from our files, not the internet.”

Retrieval over SharePoint, file shares and your systems of record, with citations.

Research and intelligence feeds

“It watches the sources we care about and tells us what’s relevant to us, every morning.”

Funding programs, bids, regulations, suppliers, competitors: whatever your team reads by hand today.

Document generation from your data

“First drafts of proposals, reports and client memos, from our past work, in our format.”

Your templates, your voice, a human edit before anything goes out.

Workflow automation with a person in the loop

“The spreadsheet process three people run becomes a system that routes, reminds and records.”

Intake, approvals, scheduling, invoicing, onboarding, compliance. Your people approve; the system does the typing.

Integrations and dashboards

“Our ERP, SharePoint and the new tools actually talk to each other, and I can see what’s happening.”

Deltek, Epicor, NetSuite, Dynamics, Microsoft 365, and whatever else you run.

Everything runs in your Azure tenant

“Our data never leaves, and nothing we build trains anyone’s model.”

You own the code, the data and the environment from day one. We put it in writing.

How we work

Three ways to work with us, in the order most companies meet them

Step 1 · The start

AI-Native Assessment and First Build

$15,000 to $25,000 fixed · 4 to 6 weeks

  • Two weeks of discovery with your department leads
  • A one-page summary of what each team does by hand
  • A ranked roadmap of 8 to 10 initiatives, with value and effort on each
  • One working tool shipped from the top of the list
  • Credited against a retainer signed within 30 days

Step 2 · Ongoing

Retained Development Function

$10,000 to $20,000 a month · 12-month term, 60-day notice

  • Run: support with response times in writing
  • Build: quarterly releases against a backlog your IT leader owns
  • Advise: a monthly roadmap meeting and a call on every AI and vendor decision
  • Documentation kept current in your repo
  • Under one loaded developer salary, with no recruiting and no ramp

Fixed-Price Projects
